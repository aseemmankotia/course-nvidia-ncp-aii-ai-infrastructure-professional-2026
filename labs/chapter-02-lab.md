# Chapter 2 Lab: Firmware, BIOS, Driver, and CUDA Stack Validation

**Estimated time:** 18 minutes

## Exercise

Read-and-understand walkthrough: interpret sample nvidia-smi topo -m and DCGM diagnostic output to validate NVLink/NVSwitch health on a simulated new DGX deployment

## Key takeaway

Firmware, driver, and CUDA must be validated and installed in strict order, and NVLink/NVSwitch topology must be confirmed with nvidia-smi/DCGM before a node is considered production-ready.
