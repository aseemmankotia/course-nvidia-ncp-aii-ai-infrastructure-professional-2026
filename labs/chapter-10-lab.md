# Chapter 10 Lab: Diagnosing GPU, NVLink, and Network Failures

**Estimated time:** 18 minutes

## Exercise

Read-and-understand walkthrough: review a sample DCGM diagnostic and NVLink error log to identify the most likely root cause of a node-level GPU fault

## Key takeaway

Effective troubleshooting isolates the fault scope (single link/node vs fabric-wide) and checks firmware/driver consistency before assuming hardware failure.
