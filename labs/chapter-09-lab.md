# Chapter 9 Lab: Slurm Scheduling and Training-vs-Inference Resource Planning

**Estimated time:** 15 minutes

## Exercise

Read-and-understand walkthrough: given a described mixed training/inference workload, decide between Slurm and Kubernetes scheduling and outline the correct resource partitioning

## Key takeaway

Slurm fits large batch training workloads while Kubernetes fits elastic inference services, and mixed clusters need explicit resource partitioning to avoid contention.
