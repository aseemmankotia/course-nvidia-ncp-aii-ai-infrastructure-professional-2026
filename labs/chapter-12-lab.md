# Chapter 12 Lab: Full Exam Simulation and Time-Management Strategy

**Estimated time:** 120 minutes

## Exercise

Full practice exam simulation: complete a 70-question timed practice set under 120-minute conditions, then review scoring broken down by domain

## Key takeaway

Consistent per-question time budgeting, a flag-and-skip strategy, and careful reading of qualifier words like 'BEST' and 'MOST likely' are essential to finishing the exam with time for review.
