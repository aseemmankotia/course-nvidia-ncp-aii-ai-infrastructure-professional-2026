# Chapter 11 Lab: Benchmarking, Performance Tuning, and Monitoring

**Estimated time:** 15 minutes

## Exercise

Read-and-understand walkthrough: review sample NCCL benchmark and DCGM utilization output to identify a performance bottleneck and recommend a tuning action

## Key takeaway

Performance issues can originate in compute, fabric, or thermal layers, so benchmarking (NCCL) and monitoring (DCGM) must be used together to correctly locate the bottleneck before tuning.
